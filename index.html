What it does

A record, not an adviser

The app keeps track of what happened. Every clinical decision stays with you and your prescriber.

Site rotation

A tappable body map shades each site by how long it has been since you used it, and suggests whichever of your allowed sites has rested longest.

Injection log

Record site, date, time, dose, and notes. Mark a day as skipped when that is what happened. Remove an entry with undo if you change your mind.

Growth tracking

Log height and weight in metric or imperial. The app calculates BMI and annualized velocity from the measurements you enter and plots them over time.

Calendar and history

A month view shows injection days and flags scheduled days that were missed. The full history is browsable in reverse order.

Summary for appointments

Generate a date-ranged PDF covering adherence, rotation, growth, dose history, and individual injections. Print or share it however you like.

Profiles and reminders

Caregivers can keep fully separate profiles, each with its own history and schedule. An optional daily reminder arrives at a time you pick.

On doses

The app never suggests a dose

Choosing a medication type sets only the unit, the route, and the sites typically used for that kind of medication. It never sets an amount. The dose is always the number your prescriber gave you, entered by you, and the app does not check it, adjust it, or comment on it.

Privacy

Your records never leave your device

There is no account to create and no server to sign in to, because there is no server.

Important

SiteCycle: Injection Tracker is a personal record-keeping tool. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. It does not verify your dose and cannot detect an error in your medication or a problem with your health.

Always follow the instructions given by your prescriber and pharmacist, and contact a qualified healthcare professional with any question about your treatment. Never start, stop, or change a medication or dose based on anything in this app.
